What makes up the 1,270 points

The CRS has three parts. With a spouse or common-law partner, the core is 530 and a spouse section adds 40; without one, the core is 570. Either way, the maximum is 1,270.

Core human capital

up to 570

Age, education, first official language and Canadian work experience.

Skill transferability

up to 100

Combinations such as education with language, or education with Canadian work experience.

Additional factors

up to 600

Factors such as a provincial nomination, French ability, a sibling in Canada or Canadian post-secondary education.

With a spouse or common-law partner, core human capital is worth up to 530, spouse or partner factors up to 40, skill transferability up to 100 and additional factors up to 600 — still 1,270 in total.

What changed on November 3, 2026

IRCC changed the Labour Market Integration factor. It now awards points for Canadian work experience in a high-wage occupation or a qualifying high-wage job offer — whichever scores higher. The two are never added together.

Common questions

What is the maximum CRS score in 2026?

The maximum is 1,270 points. Core human capital is worth up to 570 without a spouse, or 530 plus up to 40 spouse or partner points with a spouse. Skill transferability adds up to 100 and additional factors add up to 600.

What changed in the CRS on November 3, 2026?

IRCC changed the Labour Market Integration factor. It now awards points for Canadian work experience in a high-wage occupation or a qualifying high-wage job offer, whichever scores higher. The two are not added together.

What was the latest Express Entry draw?

The latest draw in MapleRoute's data is 477 points on October 28, 2026, for Transport Occupations, 2026-Version 2. IRCC paused draws from November 3 while scores were recalculated. Cutoffs change with every draw, so check IRCC's official rounds page.

Does MapleRoute calculate my official CRS score?

No. MapleRoute gives an estimate using IRCC's published criteria. IRCC calculates your official score after you submit an Express Entry profile.
